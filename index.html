About

The conference Elliptic PDEs and Beyond 2027 (EPIC 2027) aims to bring together researchers working in partial differential equations, differential geometry, and global analysis on manifolds.

The meeting will provide a platform for discussing recent developments and emerging directions in elliptic and nonlinear PDEs, with particular emphasis on their interactions with geometry, functional inequalities, and nonlocal analysis.

A central theme of the conference is the study of elliptic equations on curved and non-Euclidean spaces, where the underlying geometry plays a significant role in the analytical behaviour of solutions. The conference will also address developments in fractional and nonlocal elliptic equations, quasilinear problems, and functional inequalities, including their connections with geometric and spectral analysis.

Through invited lectures, research presentations, and dedicated sessions for young researchers, the conference seeks to encourage the exchange of ideas, foster interactions among researchers with complementary expertise, and stimulate new collaborations. It will provide an opportunity for senior Ph.D. students, postdoctoral researchers, and faculty to engage with current research developments and discuss challenging open problems.

Topics covered

  • Non-linear Elliptic Equations
  • Fractional and Nonlocal Elliptic Equations
  • Functional Inequalities: Hardy, Sobolev, and Poincaré Inequalities
  • Geometric Analysis and PDEs on Riemannian Manifolds
  • Regularity, Compactness, and Stability of Solutions
  • Spectral Properties and Variational Methods

Accommodation

Participants may avail themselves of accommodation at the International Guest House (IGH) of IIT Hyderabad on a payment basis. The organisers will block rooms at the IGH upon confirmation of participation dates by the guests or attendees.

Please note: A waiver of accommodation charges may be possible, subject to the availability of funds. In such cases, attendees willing to avail of reimbursement must submit the relevant bills to the organisers before leaving the campus.

Reaching to the campus

Local Transportation: The organizers are arranging local transport for attendees traveling to and from the airport and railway stations to IIT Hyderabad. For those who prefer to travel independently, detailed route information will be made available.

Travel Reimbursement: Partial travel support may be available, subject to the availability of funds. To be eligible for reimbursement, attendees must submit:

  • Travel tickets booked strictly via Balmer Lawrie or IRCTC.
  • A screenshot of the lowest available fare at the time of booking.
  • One-way boarding pass (for air travel).
  • A completed travel reimbursement form (provided during the conference).

The campus is on NH65 (Mumbai Highway) at Kandi. Coming from Hyderabad, you pass the main gate on your right; continue to Kandi Junction (after Kings Dhaba on the left), take a U-turn and enter through the main gate. Map apps sometimes route to the old gate, which is closed.

Inside the campus, shuttle buses run from the main gate. IITH also runs a paid campus bus from Patancheru and Miyapur at scheduled times.

Get directions in Google Maps
From Rajiv Gandhi International Airportabout 64 km

By cab, about 1.5 hours

  1. From the airport approach road, join the Outer Ring Road (ORR) at Gandiguda.
  2. Follow the ORR and take Exit 3 (Muthangi, Patancheru) towards Mumbai on NH65.
  3. Drive about 15 km on NH65. The main gate is on the right; continue to Kandi Junction and take a U-turn to enter.

By bus

  1. Take a TGSRTC Pushpak airport bus to BHEL or Miyapur (timings).
  2. Take a city bus to Patancheru (218, 218C or 219).
  3. From Patancheru, take an ordinary (not express) bus towards Sangareddy and get down at the IITH stop, opposite the main gate. Shared autos also run from Patancheru to the campus.
From Secunderabad Railway Station or JBSabout 50 km
  1. Take city bus 219, 226 or 226A to Patancheru (about 1.5 hours). These start at Jubilee Bus Station (JBS) and can be boarded at Secunderabad Railway Station.
  2. From Patancheru, take an ordinary (not express) bus towards Sangareddy and get down at the IITH stop, opposite the main gate. Shared autos also run from Patancheru to the campus.

District buses from JBS towards Sangareddy, Zaheerabad or Narayankhed also stop at IITH directly.

From Nampally Railway Station or MGBSabout 60 km

District buses from Mahatma Gandhi Bus Station (MGBS) towards Sangareddy, Zaheerabad or Narayankhed stop at IITH directly. Otherwise:

  1. Take city bus 217, 218, 225C or 225C/A from MGBS to Patancheru. From Nampally, take a city bus that starts at MGBS.
  2. From Patancheru, take an ordinary (not express) bus towards Sangareddy and get down at the IITH stop, opposite the main gate. Shared autos also run from Patancheru to the campus.
From Lingampally Railway Stationabout 25 km

Lingampally can be reached from Secunderabad by MMTS local train.

  1. Go to the BHEL Circle bus stop by shared auto or bus 217L.
  2. Take a district bus towards Sangareddy and get down at the IITH stop. Alternatively, take bus 218C to Patancheru and continue from there.
By Hyderabad Metroabout 30 km
  1. Take the Red Line to Miyapur. From Secunderabad, board at Secunderabad East (Blue Line) and change at Ameerpet.
  2. From Miyapur, take a bus to Patancheru.
  3. From Patancheru, take an ordinary (not express) bus towards Sangareddy and get down at the IITH stop, opposite the main gate. Shared autos also run from Patancheru to the campus.

A cab is the most convenient option from any of these places.
