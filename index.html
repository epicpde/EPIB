About

A three-day meeting on elliptic partial differential equations and the questions that grow out of them.

Elliptic PDEs and Beyond 2027 brings together researchers working on the analysis of elliptic equations and on the parabolic, nonlocal and geometric problems built on the same ideas. The programme includes invited lectures by leading experts, contributed talks, and dedicated time for doctoral students and early-career researchers to present their work.

The conference is organised by the Department of Mathematics, IIT Hyderabad, and will be held on campus from February 4 to 6, 2027. Talks are welcome on all aspects of elliptic PDEs, including:

  • Regularity theory
  • Nonlinear elliptic equations
  • Nonlocal and fractional operators
  • Free boundary problems
  • Calculus of variations
  • Spectral theory and eigenvalue problems
  • Homogenization
  • Numerical analysis of PDEs
